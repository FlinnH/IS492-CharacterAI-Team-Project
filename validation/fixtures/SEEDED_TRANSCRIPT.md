**Status:** EMPTY. Owner: [ ]

> Rules for writing it:
> - 20 to 30 lines, written or edited by hand so every failure is deliberate.
> - Plant 6 to 8 failures. Mix crisp ones (a fact that contradicts the spec, knowledge the character could not have) with fuzzy ones (slipping into polite assistant voice, a tone that does not fit the moment). The open question in THEORY_LENS.md Part 1 is why the mix matters.
> - Leave most lines clean, so a reviewer can raise false alarms.
> - Record every planted failure in ANSWER_KEY.md, never in this file.
