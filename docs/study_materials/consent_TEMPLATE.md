# Consent text (template)

**What the CP4 instructions expect this to hold:** the consent script, if the study needs one.

Used only if we pick Track A (decision 1 in [DECISIONS.md](../cp4/DECISIONS.md)). Copy it to `consent.md` first, and fill in the copy. If the team decides the study needs no consent text, say why in one line there.

The participant reads or hears the text below before anything else. Replace each bracket, and keep the words the same for every participant.

## The text

Thank you for helping with our class project for IS 492 at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

**What this is.** [ One sentence on what the tool does, and one on what the study is for. ]

**What you will do.** [ The tasks in a sentence, and how long the session takes. ]

**What we record.** [ What is written down or recorded. ]

**What we don't record.** [ What is left out, like your name next to your answers. ]

**Your choice.** Taking part is up to you. You can skip a question or stop at any time, and you don't have to say why. [ What happens to your answers if you stop. ]

**How we use it.** [ Where the results go, and whether anything is public. ]

**How long we keep it.** [ When the notes and any recordings are deleted. ]

**Questions.** [ Who to ask, and how to reach them. ]

Do you agree to take part? [ How the participant says yes. ]
